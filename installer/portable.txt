This is the portable version of 2014 FIFA World Cup Brazil: Recompiled.

Because this file is here, the launcher and the game keep everything in this
folder: settings, logs and the icon here, saves and the shader cache in its
"saves" folder. Nothing goes to Documents, so the copy works on its own (on a
USB stick too) and deleting this folder removes it completely.

Its saves are its own: an installed copy keeps separate ones in
Documents\2014 FIFA World Cup Brazil.
