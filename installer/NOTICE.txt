2014 FIFA World Cup Brazil: Recompiled
An unofficial PC port of the Xbox 360 game.

Unofficial fan project. Not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by
Electronic Arts, FIFA or Microsoft. 2014 FIFA World Cup Brazil, its content
and trademarks belong to their owners.

No game files are included: you need your own copy of the game (Xbox 360),
as its folder or its disc image (.iso). The first start asks for it.

This release contains code recompiled from the original game's executable; no
right to redistribute it is claimed. If a rights holder raises a valid legal
concern, it will be addressed.

This project's own code is under the BSD 3-Clause licence (LICENSE.txt); the
third-party components keep theirs (THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt).

Your saves and settings are kept in Documents\2014 FIFA World Cup Brazil
(also after an uninstall).
